# Application Prompt Modes

Append one mode to `core.md`.

## FAST

Use for routine application questions.

- Target 50–150 words unless a limit is supplied.
- Answer the question in sentence one.
- Use one strong example rather than several weak examples.
- Spend most words on evidence.
- No cover-letter introduction or conclusion.
- Minimal company context is enough unless the question asks "why us?".

## HIGH PRIORITY

Use for strong-fit / high-compensation applications.

- Parse the complete JD.
- Use verified company/product research when available.
- Select the 2–3 requirements with the highest hiring signal.
- Match each to specific evidence.
- Prefer one deep technical story plus one complementary story.
- Explain a relevant technical decision or tradeoff where space allows.
- Mention the company's concrete problem/product rather than generic enthusiasm.
- Do not conceal real gaps; frame adjacent experience accurately.
- For a short question, answer first and then prove it.

## COVER LETTER

- Target 250–350 words unless instructed otherwise.
- Keep to one page.
- 3–4 short paragraphs.
- Opening: role + concrete reason this particular work is interesting.
- Body: 1–2 strongest evidence-backed experiences, with technical specificity and results when verified.
- Explicitly connect those experiences to the team's needs.
- Close briefly and confidently.
- Do not summarize every resume position.
- Do not use "I am writing to express my interest" or ceremonial opening language.
